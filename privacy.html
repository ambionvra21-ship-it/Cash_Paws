Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

CashPaws (“the app”, “we”) is run by Von Ryan Ambion, operating from the Philippines. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. By using CashPaws you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you have any questions, you can contact us at vonryanamabion017@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and password if you sign up with email (passwords are stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider), your name and email from Google if you use Google sign-in, or a random ID if you continue as a guest.
  • Profile. Your display name, avatar, referral code, and the code of the person who invited you.
  • Activity. Your daily step total from your phone’s step sensor (we receive daily totals, not a record of where you walk), login streaks, missions, spins, the number of rewarded ads you watched, and your coin balance.
  • Withdrawal details. The payment method (GCash, Maya or One), the account number or email you enter for it, the amount, the currency, and the status of your request.
  • Advertising and device data. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

How we use it

  • To run the app, calculate rewards and keep your balance.
  • To check for fraud, fake steps, and duplicate accounts.
  • To review and pay your withdrawal requests.
  • To show ads, which pay for the app.
  • To show the weekly leaderboard. Other players can see your display name, avatar and weekly coins. They cannot see your email or payment details.

Permissions

The app asks for Physical activity access to count your steps. You can say no, but the Walk feature will not work. The app also needs internet access.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers to run the app: Supabase (sign-in, database and hosting) and Google (AdMob ads, and Google sign-in if you choose it). When we pay a withdrawal, we send the money to the account you entered. We may also share information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app under Profile, then Delete account. This removes your profile, coins, referral code and withdrawal history. Deletion is not possible while a withdrawal is pending. Copies in backups are removed as the backups rotate.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules let each person read only their own account data. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

CashPaws is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting us, and you can delete your account in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your local data protection law. We will respond within a reasonable time.

International processing

Our providers may process data in other countries. We choose providers that protect it with appropriate safeguards.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change is important.
